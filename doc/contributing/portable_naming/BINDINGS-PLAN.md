# The MEOS functions every binding binds

[Back to the index](00-INDEX.md) · [Implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) · [The SQL functions MEOS answers](WRAPPERS-PLAN.md)

The MEOS-API catalog `meos-idl.json` is the one place a MEOS header is parsed, and every binding is
a projection of it down to its lowest layer. This document states the one rule by which every
binding chooses the MEOS functions it binds and the declarations it calls them through, where each
binding stands against it, and the pull requests that bring each one to it.

## The rule

1. **A binding binds every catalog function of an enabled family.** The family of a function is the
   catalog's `family` field. Every family is enabled by default, a build drops one with
   `-D<FAMILY>=OFF`, and the core is always bound. An entry the catalog flags `vendored` (the
   pgpointcloud and PostgreSQL code MobilityDB carries) stays out. No binding keeps a list of
   headers or of families: a new family reaches it with the next catalog.
2. **A binding has two layers.** Its low-level layer (the FFI, sys or flat functions) binds every
   function rule 1 selects, the internal ones included. Its user-facing layer (the SQL functions of
   DuckDB, Spark and Flink, the object layer of Go and Python) leaves out the functions of the
   `meos_internal` group.
3. **A function is declared from its catalog signature.** A binding never includes a header to
   obtain a prototype, never reads the content of a header, and never lists header files; a header
   is included only for the types it defines. Whether MobilityDB installs a header therefore never
   decides what a binding reaches: the 221 functions of the internal `pgtypes.h` umbrella, which
   `libmeos.so` exports while the install leaves the header behind, are bound like any other.
4. **A binding generates from the catalog of the MEOS it builds against**, as the `provision-meos`
   action of MEOS-API derives both from one MobilityDB commit. No binding scans headers or symbols
   to keep only what its build declares.

The reference is JMEOS: `codegen/src/main/java/FunctionsGenerator.java` binds every catalog function
of an enabled family through JNR, by its symbol, and keeps no header list; its enums leave out the
vendored ones and those of `meos_internal.h`. Spark and Flink, generated by JMEOS
`tools/codegen_spark_udfs.py` and `tools/codegen_jvm.py`, bind the symbols of the JMEOS jar and leave
out the `meos_internal` group.

|---|---|---|---|
| JMEOS | every catalog function of an enabled family | none: JNR binds the symbols of `libmeos.so` | follows it; its functions keep the vendored entries its enums leave out |
| Spark, Flink, Kafka | the symbols of the JMEOS jar, less the `meos_internal` group | the JMEOS jar | follow it |
| MEOS.NET | the non-vendored catalog, grouped by `file` | none: P/Invoke binds the symbols | follows rules 1, 3 and 4; the family switches are to confirm |
| GoMEOS | the functions of 13 headers listed in `HEADER_FILES` of `tools/codegen.py` | the `#include` of those headers | rules 1 and 3: `meos_raster.h`, `meos_s2cell.h` and `pg_text.h` are not listed, so 93 methods of its object layer have no flat function |
| MobilityDuck | the functions of 16 headers listed in `TU_C_HEADERS` of `tools/codegen_duck_udfs.py`, kept only where `header_symbols` finds them by a regular expression over the header text | the `#include` of those headers | rules 1, 3 and 4: the raster, raquet and base-type functions outside the list are not bound |

| MEOS.js, MobilityNebula | not yet read | not yet read | to check |

## GEOS and GSL, which MEOS does not call

MEOS calls neither GEOS nor GSL: its random draws come from the `pg_prng` generators of
PostgreSQL, and over MobilityDB master `a23e01a60b` `libmeos.so` neither needs nor calls a GEOS or
a GSL symbol. GEOS still loads with it through the GDAL it links: the GDAL a platform packages
(`libgdal-dev` on Ubuntu, `gdal-devel` on Fedora, `mingw-w64-ucrt-x86_64-gdal` on MSYS2) links
GEOS. A binding therefore installs, builds and links neither, and builds libmeos against GDAL 3.12.0,
the version MobilityDuck takes, built from its source in its standard configuration with
`-DGDAL_USE_GEOS=OFF`; PROJ and its sqlite3 stay, as the raster family needs them. The RTOS build of
MEOS loads no GDAL at all: it is `-DRASTER=OFF`, without PROJ.

## Decisions this plan rests on

- The ecosystem targets 1.4 only, with no backward compatibility for 1.3 (the owner, 2026-10-27).
- GEOS and GSL leave every binding, and the hosted bindings link a GDAL built from source without
  GEOS (the owner, 2026-10-27).
- The catalog is the one source of every binding, its FFI layer included, and only MEOS-API parses
  headers (the owner, 2026-07-10).
