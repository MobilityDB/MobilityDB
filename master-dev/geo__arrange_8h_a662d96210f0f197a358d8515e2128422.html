Return the rings bounding the union of surfaces bounded by segments and arcs, as surfaces, and the linework and the points beside them that no surface covers.

As arrange_union_surfaces, the linework arranged with the two unions of the last merge: a segment of it winds around nothing, so it bounds no ring, and its pieces where no surface winds are answered; a point is answered where it lies outside every surface and off every segment of the linework (arr_lines_points)

Parameters
[in]edges,soff,nsurfThe surfaces, as arrange_union_surfaces reads them, turning counterclockwise
[in]lines,nlinesThe segments of the linework, of the input
[in]pts,nptsThe points
[out]rings,nrings,ownerThe surfaces, as arrange_union_surfaces answers them
[out]loutWhere lines is given, its pieces no surface covers, as segments (ArrangePiece); released by the caller
[out]pkeepWhere pts is given, whether each point lies off the surfaces and the linework
Returns
ARR_ANSWERED, or the question left unanswered
