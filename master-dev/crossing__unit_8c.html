Detailed Description

Test of the exact crossings of float segments, tfloatsegm_side, tnumbersegm_side, tfloatsegm_crossing and tnumbersegm_crossing.

Each row states a segment whose exact crossing is known as a rational: a crossing on a whole microsecond, a tie half-way between two microseconds in both directions and from an odd and an even start, a crossing whose nearest microsecond is a bound, two segments crossing at a seventh of a day, a segment longer than 2^60 microseconds, and values whose differences overflow the doubles.
