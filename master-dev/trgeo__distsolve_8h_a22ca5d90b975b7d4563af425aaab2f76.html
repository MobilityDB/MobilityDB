Find the solutions of f(t) = level in [lo, hi] at which f crosses the level.

Between two consecutive roots, f does not change sign, because distfun_roots() finds all the roots. Thus the function reads the sign of f - level at the middle of each gap between roots. A root is a crossing if the gaps before and after it have opposite signs. A gap in which |f - level| <= ftol gives no sign, and a group of roots with such gaps between them is one root at its first member.

A root at lo or hi has no gap on one side, thus it is never a crossing. A touch has the same sign on the two sides, thus it is never a crossing.

Parameters
[out]rootsCrossings, in increasing order
[out]dirsDirection of each crossing: 1 if f goes up through the level and -1 if it goes down
Returns
Number of crossings, or one of the negative results of distfun_roots()
