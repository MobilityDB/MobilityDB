Detailed Description

The integer addresses of the points of the outline of an H3 cell, read from the core library of H3 vendored under h3lib/.

Decision D8 of the cell walks states each point of the outline of an H3 cell as the double nearest the degrees of the value H3's formula gives it. The inputs of that formula are integers: a face of the icosahedron, the resolution of the substrate grid and a point of its lattice, or for an extra vertex, the crossing of the segment between two points of the lattice with an edge of the face. h3_vertex.c reads them from the vendored library, whose headers it alone includes, so this header names them in the integer types of pg_basetypes.h. A point of the lattice is given as (2x, 2y / sqrt 3) of the planar point (x, y) H3 computes (_ijkToHex2d): two integers, as are the ends of a face edge.
