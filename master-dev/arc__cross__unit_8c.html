Detailed Description

Test of the proper crossings of a segment and an arc and of two arcs that relate reads, arcsegm_cross and arcarc_cross of meos/include/geo/geo_funcs.h.

Each case is built so that its answer is known in closed form: the arcs lie on circles of radius 5 through points whose coordinates are doubles (the 3-4-5 triangle), and every offset is a power of two, so each crossing lies where the construction puts it. A crossing is proper where the two edges pass through each other at a point interior to both; a tangency, a meeting at an end of either edge and a meeting outside the span of an arc are not. Each case runs at the scales 2^-40, 1 and 2^40, which are exact on doubles, so an answer depends on no magnitude. The edges are the ones relate reads, from geom_extract_edges.
