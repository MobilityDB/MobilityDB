bool mest_key_answers ( int16  flags,
StrategyNumber  strategy 
)

Return true if a key of a multi-entry index may answer a strategy.

The key of the box of a whole value (mest_keys_whole) answers the operators reading the interior or a face of the box of the value, which no piece witnesses: the overlaps, the contains and the adjacency. The keys of the pieces answer every other operator, each holding on the box of a value iff on the box of one of its pieces or of every one. A key over the keys of several structures stands for both and answers any

Parameters
[in]flagsFlags of the key
[in]strategyStrategy number of the operator
