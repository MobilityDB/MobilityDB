Detailed Description

Test of the border tile of the QUADBIN split of a temporal point.

For every zoom from 1 to a maximum, the argument of the program (10 by default), and every interior bound of the grid, a segment ends on the bound or one ulp on each side of it. A tile holds its west and north bounds, so the tile across the bound holds the end when the end lies on its side or on the bound, and holds nothing but the border of the trajectory when the end lies on the bound. The split of tgeompoint_quadbin_split holds that tile when it holds more than the border, and when it holds the border alone only if borderInc is true, as the split of a temporal point by a space grid lays its tiles. The fractional tile coordinates, read in doubles, put a bound on the wrong side: the longitude 2^-1074 at zoom 1 lies in column 1, which the split left out, and the latitude of a row bound kept the row holding the border alone.
