Detailed Description

A double and a bound on its distance to the real number it computes.

The bounds follow those of polynomial_sign_pairs: with u = DBL_EPSILON / 2, a rounding of a result c moves it by at most u |c|, and each bound is inflated by 1 + 16 DBL_EPSILON for its own rounding. A result whose magnitude is below 2^-900 or not finite, where underflow could lose its error, makes the evaluation decline
