Return the sign of the comparison of the parameters at which a segment reaches two clip constraints.

The parameter of the plane axis = h is (h - c1) / (c2 - c1) on that axis. The difference of two of them on axes a and b, times (c2_a - c1_a) (c2_b - c1_b), is the cross product of the vectors (h_a, h_b) - (c1_a, c1_b) and (c2_a, c2_b) - (c1_a, c1_b), whose sign is decided on the input doubles (cross_product_sign). The parameters 0 and 1 compare with that of a plane as the bound compares with c1 and c2. No parameter is computed.

Parameters
[in]c1,c2Coordinates of the start and end points of the segment
[in]a,bConstraints, a plane on an axis along which the segment moves
Returns
-1, 0 or 1 as the parameter of a is less than, equal to or greater than that of b
