static GSERIALIZED * trgeo_convex_sweep ( const GSERIALIZED *  a,
const GSERIALIZED *  b 
)
static

Return the region a convex body covers between two of its placements.

Every point of a convex body travels within the hull of the two placements, each vertex moving along a straight segment, so that hull is what the body covers between them. The hull also contains the two placements it spans, so a sweep replaces them rather than joining them.
