Detailed Description

Test of the relationships of two areas bounded by arcs that meet near an end of an arc, geom_disjoint2d, geom_touches, geom_contains and geom_covers.

Each case is built so that its answer is known, as arc_cases of arc_cross_unit.c builds its own: the lens of the arc of the circle of radius 5 about the origin from (4, -3) through (5, 0) to (4, 3), closed by its chord, against a lens or a half disc of the circle of radius 5 about (c, 0). With c = 8 - 2^-44 the lens from (c - 4, 3) through (c - 5, 0) to (c - 4, -3) lies 2^-44 left of the first, the two chords parallel, and the two are disjoint. With c = 8 + 2^-44 the half disc left of the diameter from (c, 5) to (c, -5) crosses the first lens 2^-44 inside the ends of its arc: their interiors overlap and neither contains the other. Two squares 2^-44 apart are the straight control. Each runs at the scales 2^-40, 1 and 2^40, which are exact on doubles, so an answer depends on no magnitude.
