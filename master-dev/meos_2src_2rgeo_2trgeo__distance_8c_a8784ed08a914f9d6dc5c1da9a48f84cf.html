Set h to a function whose sign is the sign of the distance of the piece o minus the distance of the piece c, and htol to the tolerance of its values.

The distance of a vertex is sqrt(V) and the distance of an edge is G / L, with G not negative in the region of the edge, see DistPiece. Thus, with the squares and the lengths that keep the sign,
