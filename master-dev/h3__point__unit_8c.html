Test of the planar cell holding a position of meos/src/h3/h3_geo.c.

A planar cell is its straight-edged outline (h3_cell_outline), holding its interior and, by the rule of the cell walks, its north and west sides (h3_outline_holds_point), the cell around a pole the region between its ring and the pole. First, the rows of h3_point_rows.inc, each a position whose cell the oracle of the plan of the cell walks decides in rationals and the library, latLngToCell, names another: the point function h3_point_cell names the oracle's cell. Then, for every cell of the resolutions 0 to a maximum, the argument of the program (1 by default): each vertex of its outline, and the positions one ulp east, west, north and south of it, lie in exactly one of the cells meeting there, which h3_point_cell names; each vertex of the boundary the library gives the cell, where the outline and the library part, lies in the cell h3_point_cell names and in no other cell beside it.
