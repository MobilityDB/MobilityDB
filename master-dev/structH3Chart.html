Detailed Description

Outline of a cell read in the chart of a segment.

Vertex i of the chart, for any integer i, is the vertex i mod n of the outline, its longitude carrying the exact offset xo[i mod n] + s, and around a pole wrap more for each turn of the ring, so that the edge from each vertex to the next runs along the ring in the chart of the segment, the ring around a pole repeated every 360 degrees. No offset is added to a longitude, where it would round.
