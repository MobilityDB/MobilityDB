Detailed Description

A piece of the distance in a segment: an interval of time in which the walk follows one feature, or in which the bodies overlap.

For a vertex, f is the squared distance and len is 0. For an edge, f is the cross function, which is not negative in the region of the edge, and len is the length of the edge, so that the distance is f / len. In an overlap, the distance is zero.
