int h3_cell_vertexes ( uint64  cell,
uint64 *  out,
uint64 *  across 
)

Set out to the vertex indices of the cell cell in the order of cellToVertexes, the last H3_NULL for a pentagon, and, where across is not NULL, across to the cell across each edge, the edge k running from the vertex k to the next; return their number, 5 or 6, or -1 for an index that is not a cell.

The rule of cellToVertex of vertex.c, step for step: a vertex belongs to the lowest index of the three cells meeting there, and is numbered in that cell. cellToVertexes reads it vertex by vertex, so it states the rotations of the cell (vertexRotations) twice per vertex and each neighbour twice, once per vertex of the edge they share; here the rotations are stated once and each neighbour once (cell_neighbour). The cell across the edge k is the neighbour in the direction of the vertex k (directionForVertexNum), the left neighbour of that rule
