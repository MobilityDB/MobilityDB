Detailed Description

The face coordinates (_h3ToFaceIjk) and the vertex rotations (vertexRotations) of the cells read last, per thread, by the cell, as quadbin_row_latitude keeps the latitudes of the QUADBIN rows: the recipes, the center and the vertices of a cell read them, and so do the cells around it, each of which owns some of its vertices.
