Detailed Description

The question a caller asks of a DE-9IM matrix: one or more patterns, any of which matching answers it.

A relationship reads a handful of the nine cells, and the patterns deciding it spell out which: contains is T*****FF*, touches holds when any of FT*******, F**T***** or F***T**** does. The engine computes the cells the patterns name and stops once the answer is known, as the GEOS relate engine does, instead of computing all nine cells whatever the question
