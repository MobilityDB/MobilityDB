Detailed Description

Test of the tile holding a position of meos/src/quadbin/quadbin.c.

For every zoom from 1 to a maximum, the argument of the program (10 by default), a position on each bound of the tiles, the meridians and the parallels of the grid, and one ulp on each side of it, lies in the tile whose box, as quadbin_cell_bounding_box states it, holds it by the rule of the cell walks: a tile holds its west and north bounds (quadbin_cell_edge_held), the first row every latitude above the limit of the grid and the last row every latitude below it, the poles included, their boxes reaching the poles (decision D5). The point function quadbin_point_to_cell and both ends of the planar walk quadbin_segment_cells name that tile. The fractional coordinates of the Mercator projection, read in doubles, put the position beside a bound in the tile across it: at zoom 1 the longitude -2^-1074 in column 1 and the latitude 2^-1074 in row 1.
