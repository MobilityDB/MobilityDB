Detailed Description

The double nearest the sine and cosine of input degrees.

The cosine and sine of a double read as degrees are transcendental except at the multiples of 30 (Niven), so no finite computation states them; the double nearest each is decided exactly all the same. The angle is reduced to [0, 45] by subtractions that are exact (Sterbenz), the rational values are read from their table, and every other value is enclosed in an interval of fixed-point integers: pi by Machin's formula, the sine and the cosine by their Taylor series, each an alternating series of decreasing terms whose next term bounds what it leaves out, every product and quotient rounded outward. The one search for the nearest double, nearest_double_exact, reads the enclosure through its side function trig_side, which raises the precision until the interval lies clear of the midpoint the search asks about. The geodetic position of decision D6 of the cell walks is the vector of the doubles nearest cos(lat) cos(lon), cos(lat) sin(lon) and sin(lat) (lonlat_vector_nearest)
