Enclose the sine or the cosine of a degrees, 0 < a <= 45, at the scale 2^-S in [lo, hi].

The radians x = a pi / 180 are read from the enclosure of pi and the exact double a; the Taylor series of the sine (x - x^3/3! + ...) and of the cosine (1 - x^2/2! + ...) alternate with decreasing terms as x < 1, so the sum stops at a term of at most one unit, which bounds the rest
