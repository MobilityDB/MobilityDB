Return a raster keeping the pixels of another that a geometry covers.

The bands kept are read in turn against a mask the geometry is burnt into, as the PostGIS ST_Clip reads them. A pixel the geometry does not cover answers the nodata value of its band: the value nodata states, one for every band or one per band with the last standing for the bands after it, or otherwise the nodata value of the band, and the smallest value its pixel type holds where the band states none. When crop is true the result carries the extent the two share, and otherwise it carries the extent of the subject

Parameters
[in]rastRaster to clip
[in]bandsNumbers of the bands to keep, starting at 1, in the order of the result, or NULL for every band
[in]nbandsNumber of elements of bands
[in]gsGeometry to clip it to
[in]nodataNodata values of the bands of the result, or NULL for the nodata value of each band
[in]nnodataNumber of elements of nodata
[in]cropTrue to reduce the result to the extent the raster and the geometry share
[in]touchedTrue to keep every pixel the geometry touches, false to keep the pixels whose centre it covers
Returns
