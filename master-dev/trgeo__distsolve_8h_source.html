45/*****************************************************************************
46 * The function family
47 *
48 * A function of the family is
49 *
50 * F(t) = SUM_k [ (a1_k*t + a0_k) * cos(w_k*t)
51 * + (b1_k*t + b0_k) * sin(w_k*t) ]
52 * + c2*t^2 + c1*t + c0
53 *
54 * Each term has phase zero and a frequency that is not negative. In one
55 * temporal segment, the frequencies are wA, wB and wA - wB, where wA and wB
56 * are the rotations of the two bodies. Thus a function has at most three
57 * terms. If the bodies do not rotate, the function is a quadratic.
58 *****************************************************************************/
59
