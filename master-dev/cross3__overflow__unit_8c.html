Detailed Description

Test of the signs in 3D whose cross products overflow the doubles, vector_triple_sign and geom_dwithin3d.

A face a = 0, b = (X, -X, 0), c = (X, X, 2X) and a segment from (3X/4, X/4, X/4) to (X/4, -X/4, 3X/4), which crosses the face at (X/2, 0, X/2), a point inside it at the weights (1/2, 1/4, 1/4), so the two are within any distance at every scale X. From X = 2^512 on, every product of two components of (c - a) x (p - a) is past the largest double, and the two of each component are infinities of one sign whose difference is not zero. Each scale is a power of two, so every coordinate is exact on doubles and an answer depends on no magnitude. The orientations are rows whose exact sign is known, read with the vectors scaled by 2^-600, exactly, where no product overflows.
