double geom_distance2d_disc ( const double *  c,
double  r,
const GSERIALIZED *  gs 
)

Return the double nearest the distance of a geometry of straight edges to the disc of centre c and radius r, 2D, or -1 for a geometry of another type.

The disc of geom_distance2d_exact: the distance of the centre to the geometry less the radius, 0 where the disc meets the geometry. Each segment, a point being a segment of no length, answers the double nearest its distance less the radius (point_segment_distance_offset_exact), and rounding is monotone, so the least of these doubles is the answer. The coordinates are read in place in one pass. The search starts from the first segment and passes over a segment whose two ends lie beyond one side of the window of the centre plus or minus the radius plus the least found, widened past the rounding of its bounds, or whose squared distance in doubles, within a few units in the last place of the largest coordinate of the exact one where the coordinates are within a factor 2^400 of 1 (read from the box of the geometry), lies farther than that by 2^-40 of that coordinate. The disc meets a polygon whose interior holds its centre, decided exactly by the even-odd crossings of its rings, counted in the same pass as geom_distance_ring_contains counts them

Parameters
[in]cCentre, two coordinates
[in]rRadius, not negative
[in]gsGeometry, not empty
