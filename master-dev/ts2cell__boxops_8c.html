Detailed Description

Bounding box functions for the temporal S2 cell type.

An S2 cell is defined on the sphere, so a temporal S2 cell carries a GEODETIC STBox: X and Y set, the geodetic flag set, no Z, and T taken from the time span — the pattern tgeogpoint and th3index follow, where the Web-Mercator tquadbin carries a planar one.

The static helper converts a single cell to a geodetic box through the derivation the cell-index scaffolding shares, which adds to the vertex extent the latitude a geodesic edge reaches, the pole a cell holding one reaches, and the full longitude range a cell holding a pole or crossing the antimeridian spans. The per-instant wrapper adds the timestamp, the array helper takes the union, and the expand helper is what sequence construction calls.
