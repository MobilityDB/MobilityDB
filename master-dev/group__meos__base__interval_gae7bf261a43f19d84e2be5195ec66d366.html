Return in the last three arguments the months, the days and the microseconds of an interval.

A binding converts an interval to the duration of its host through this function and back through interval_from_parts, as timestamptz_to_unix_us converts a timestamptz. The three parts are those the interval holds: a day of an interval is not 24 hours across a change of the offset of a time zone, nor a month a number of days, so a host duration counting time alone states an interval whose months and days are zero.
