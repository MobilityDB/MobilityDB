Detailed Description

Test of the signs on coordinates shifted by exact offsets of meos/src/geo/geo_funcs.c.

cross_product_sign_offset is checked on orientations whose x coordinates are shifted by multiples of 360, as a cell of a grid is read across the antimeridian, against the signs computed in exact rationals: on the witness rows the sign on the doubles the shift rounds to differs from the exact one, and the test fails if it does not, so that the rows keep exercising the rounding; on the control rows the two agree. Then point_in_ring_offset_exact locates points in a ring straddling the antimeridian, inside, outside, on an edge and at a vertex.
