◆ tnpoint_meeting_band()

static double tnpoint_meeting_band ( double  bound1,
double  bound2 
)
static

Return the distance within which the points the routes of two temporal network points construct at one instant are one place, given the largest bounds of construction of the routes of the two values.

A position along a route is a point computed in doubles, as npoint_to_geompoint computes it, so two network points at one place of the map, a crossing of their routes, give two points apart by the rounding of their coordinates, and the exact distance between them is almost never zero. Each lies within the bound of construction of its route (route_construction_bound) of the place, so the two lie within the sum of the bounds of the routes of the two values, which is the distance, with no absolute term

Parameters
[in]bound1,bound2Largest bounds of construction of the routes of the two values, which tnpoint_tgeompoint_bound returns with their positions
