Detailed Description

A convex polygon, a segment or a point, relative to its rotation center.

The vertices of a polygon are in counterclockwise order, and no vertex repeats or lies on the line of its two neighbors. Edge i goes from vertex i to vertex i + 1. A segment has two vertices and two edges in opposite directions. A point has one vertex and no edges.
