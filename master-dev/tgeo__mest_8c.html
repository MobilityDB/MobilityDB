Detailed Description

Multi-entry support of the temporal geometries and geographies: the keys of a value are the spatiotemporal boxes MEOS splits it into.

The extraction methods of their multi-entry classes, as temporal_mest.c holds those of the temporal types and numbers; each calls the internal twin of a splitter or a merge of the temporal geometries
