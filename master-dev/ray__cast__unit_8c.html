Detailed Description

Test of the location of a point in an area that the ray casts of the geometry engine answer: the inside test of the nearest approach distance, dist_geom_point_inside, and the point in polygon test of the spatial relationships, meos_point_in_polygon.

Each case is built so that its answer is known in closed form, as segment_cases of arc_cross_unit.c builds its own. A point at the centre of a disc, or 3/5 of the way to its circle, is inside it, so its nearest approach distance is 0, and a point 10 from the centre of a disc of radius 5 is 5 from it; each runs at the scales 2^-40, 1 and 2^40, which are exact on doubles, so an answer depends on no magnitude. The triangles share an edge and a point a few units in the last place off its midpoint, the third vertex on either side, so the point is inside one and outside the other; its side of the edge is the sign of a determinant of degree two in the coordinates, read exactly on rationals.
