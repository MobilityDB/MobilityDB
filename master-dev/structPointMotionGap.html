Detailed Description

Return the sign of the least, over a segment, of the gap between a disc moving linearly and a point, less a distance d + dlo, exactly.

The gap |w(f)| - L(f) and the quadratic q(f) of point_motion_dwithin_coeffs have the same sign at every fraction, L being not negative, so the sign of the least gap is that of the least of q over [0, 1]: at an end, or at the vertex -B / A where the parabola is convex and the vertex lies inside, there (A C - B^2) / A
