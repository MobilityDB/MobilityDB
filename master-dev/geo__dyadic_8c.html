Detailed Description

The exact arithmetic of the geometry engine: the representations, the tiers, the expression ladder, the searches and the exact signs.

The algorithms of geo_funcs.c read it through geo_dyadic.h. Every value it holds is a dyadic rational, a double, a pair or an expansion of doubles, or a DyadicWide
