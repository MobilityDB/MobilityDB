Detailed Description

The point arrays and the faces of a geometry in 3D, read in place on its serialized form (geom_faces_init)

The point arrays reference the serialized coordinates, as geom_distance_gs_arrays reads them, held with the faces in the arrays on the stack of the structure until they outgrow them, as geom_distance2d_exact holds its point arrays
