Detailed Description

Test the split of a temporal point by a space grid on the bounds of its tiles.

Every tile contains its lower bound and excludes its upper one, and holds exactly the microsecond instants whose exact position lies in it, so each instant of a trajectory belongs to exactly one tile. With borderInc false, a tile starting on the upper bound of the extent of the trajectory is left out, and an instant whose position lies on that bound is held by no tile.

The program splits by unit tiles trajectories lying on, and within a few ulps of, an upper tile bound, and random trajectories whose coordinates lie on a quarter of a tile and up to 4 ulps off it, with and without the bit matrix, and verifies at the input instants, at the bounds, the midpoints and the neighbours of the bounds of every fragment, and around every crossing, that the instant is held by exactly the tile of its exact position. That tile is decided on the input doubles: the position at the instant t of the segment from a at t1 to b at t1 + d lies at or above the bound k when (a - k) d + (b - a) (t - t1) is not negative, whose sign is read exactly from the rounding errors of its differences and products, so the program shares no arithmetic with the split.
