int polynomial_sign_pairs ( const ExactSum *  sums,
const PolyTerm *  terms,
int  nterms 
)

Return the sign of a polynomial in exact sums where its evaluation in pairs of doubles carries it, and 2 where it cannot tell.

The second filter of polynomial_sign_exact. Each exact sum is read as a pair, its largest component and the rounded sum of the others, within 2 u of those others, u being DBL_EPSILON / 2. Each term is a pair, its coefficient times its sums: the product of two pairs is the exact product of their larger parts (two_product) plus the rounded cross terms, within 8.1 u^2 of the product of the larger parts, besides the errors the factors carry, which the bound carries through the product. The terms are added into a pair (two_sum), each rounding of the smaller part adding u of it. Where the pair stands clear of twice the bound, every bound inflated for its own rounding, its sign is the polynomial's. A product of the larger parts outside 2^-900 to 2^900, which could lose its error to underflow, declines. Mirrors incircle_sign_filter

Parameters
[in]sumsExact sums the terms refer to
[in]terms,ntermsTerms of the polynomial and their number
