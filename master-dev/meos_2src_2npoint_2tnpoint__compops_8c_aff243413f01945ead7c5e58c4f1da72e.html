◆ tnpoint_meeting_band()

static double tnpoint_meeting_band ( const STBox *  box1,
const STBox *  box2 
)
static

Return the distance within which the points the routes of two temporal network points construct at one instant are one place.

A position along a route is a point computed in doubles, as npoint_to_geompoint computes it, so two network points at one place of the map, a crossing of their routes, give two points apart by the rounding of their coordinates, and the exact distance between them is almost never zero. The distance is the band of a constructed point the exact computation of MEOS admits, as geompoint_route_band reads it for a point on a route and geogpoint_meeting_band for the meeting of two geography points: MEOS_GEOM_TOLERANCE plus four units of rounding of the largest coordinate of the two values, which no two points genuinely apart are within

Parameters
[in]box1,box2Spatiotemporal boxes of the two values
