Detailed Description

State of the aggregate summarizing the pixels of rasters.

The shape of PostGIS's state of ST_SummaryStatsAgg: the band read and whether nodata is left out, what the pixels read amount to, and the running mean and sum of squared deviations from which the standard deviation is taken in one pass
