Detailed Description

Test of the vertices of an S2 cell of meos/src/s2cell/s2cell.c.

A vertex is the corner of the cube, exact: its coordinates on the face are the rationals of the quadratic transform at a dyadic index, and its longitude and latitude are the doubles nearest the degrees of that exact corner (s2cell_cell_vertices, vector_lon_nearest, vector_lat_nearest; decision D7 of the cell walks). The rows are cells whose vertices a judge in MPFR and CGAL::Gmpq, sharing no code with MEOS, states, each one whose vertices read as the cube coordinates rounded in doubles and libm's atan2 differ, or one touching the antimeridian or a pole. A second pass reads every other vertex again from its corner as a vector of integers doubled, which lies beyond the 2^62 the bounded pairs of doubles read (ddb_vector_lon_nearest), so the enclosure in fixed point answers it (degree_angle_enclose), and it must give the same degrees.
