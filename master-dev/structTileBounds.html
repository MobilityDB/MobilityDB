Detailed Description

Bounds of the tiles of a grid along each dimension, as the grid lays its tiles.

Along a spatial dimension, the lower bound of a tile is the one of the tile before it plus the size, as stbox_tile_state_next advances, and its upper bound is its lower bound plus the size, as stbox_tile_state_set builds it: the upper bound of a tile is the lower bound of the next one, bit for bit. The bounds of the time dimension are integers, exact
