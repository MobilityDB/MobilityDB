Detailed Description

A point of a face of an icosahedral gnomonic grid, the vertex of an H3 cell of decision D8 of the cell walks (geo_trig.c)

The constants are the decimal literals of the grid read exactly; a point of the lattice is (2x, 2y / sqrt 3) of its planar point (x, y)
