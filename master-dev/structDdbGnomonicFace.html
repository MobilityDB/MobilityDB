Detailed Description

The constants of a face of an icosahedral gnomonic grid in bounded pairs of doubles: the center c, the unnormalized north at it n, n x c, |n|, |n| c, the sine and the cosine of the azimuth of the first axis less the turn of the angle, the gnomonic length of the unit, sqrt 3 and sqrt 7, and the scale k of each resolution, on the grid of the resolution and on its substrate, stated where first read (ddb_gnomonic_scale)
