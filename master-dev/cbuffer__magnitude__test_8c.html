Detailed Description

The relationships of two circular buffers whose squared distances leave the range of a double, judged by the whole numbers.

The relationships read the sign of the squared distance between the centres less the square of a sum of the radii. Where the coordinates are subnormal, those squares lie below the smallest double, and where they exceed 2^512, beyond the largest; each case below states its sign on whole multiples of a power of two.
