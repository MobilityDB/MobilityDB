Detailed Description

Test of the order of the crossings of the planar walk of meos/src/quadbin/quadbin.c.

A planar segment crossing a column bound and a row bound near the corner where they meet reaches one of them first, and passes through the tile beside the corner on that side, or passes through the corner itself. quadbin_segment_cells decides it by the side of the segment the corner lies on, a sign of a cross product on the input doubles. The rows are segments through the corners of the tiles of the zooms 2 to 8 and one ulp of an end beside them, each the walk the base answered wrongly, with the tiles an exact oracle in rational arithmetic, sharing no code with MEOS, states: the parameters of the two crossings round to the same double or to a reversed order, and the base passed through the corner where the segment passes beside it, or the reverse. The witness: at zoom 2, the segment from (45, 33.25...) to (-45 - 2^-47, -33.25...) reaches the meridian 0 before the equator, and walks through (1, 1), not through (2, 2).
