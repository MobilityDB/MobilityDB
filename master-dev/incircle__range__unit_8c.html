Detailed Description

Test of the incircle sign over the whole range of the doubles, incircle_sign.

The circle through A = (1, 0), B = (0, 1) and C = (-1, 0), taken counterclockwise, is the unit circle, so the sign is 1 for D = (0, 1/2) inside it, -1 for D = (0, 2) outside, 0 for D = (0, -1) on it, 1 for the double above -1 and -1 for the double below -1, one unit in the last place inside and outside. A second circle through (3, 1), (1, 3) and (-1, 1), of centre (1, 1) and radius 2, holds (1, -1) on it. The determinant is homogeneous of degree 4 in the coordinate differences, so scaling the four points by 2^e leaves its sign unchanged; every scale from 2^-1020 to 2^1020 keeps each coordinate an exact double, and the rows of a scale whose products of four differences overflow or underflow the doubles answer as those of 2^0. The last row, four points in general position whose sign GMP reads as 1, has a determinant whose evaluation in doubles, near 2^-268, rounds to the least subnormal with its bound underflowing to 0.
