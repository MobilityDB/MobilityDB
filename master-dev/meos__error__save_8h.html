Detailed Description

Saving the errors of a parse instead of raising them.

The twin of the ErrorSaveContext of PostgreSQL: a caller that asks whether a string is a value of a type, as the item methods of a jsonpath do, parses it with its errors saved and reads whether one occurred, rather than having the parser raise its own error.
