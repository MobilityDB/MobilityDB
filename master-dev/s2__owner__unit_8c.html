Detailed Description

Test of the owner of a boundary point of an S2 cell of meos/src/s2cell/s2cell.c.

For every cell of the levels 0 to a maximum, the argument of the program (4 by default), each edge of its ring is held by exactly one of the two cells sharing it (s2cell_cell_edge_held), and each vertex by exactly one of the cells meeting there (s2cell_cell_vertex_held), the cube edges, the cube corners and the poles included. Each edge a cell holds faces its lower index coordinates, and the north pole and the two cube corners no cell holds as its low corner have the owners the rule of the cell walks names (doc/contributing/cells/RULE.md). A corner is named by its exact point of the cube, scaled to integers: the cube coordinate 2 a / n - 1 of the index a of a level of n cells a side is read as 2 a - n, which names the same point since the projection of the face is symmetric about its centre. The test also checks that the vertex k of s2cell_cell_vertices is the corner the two functions read as k: it lies nearer that corner, placed by the quadratic projection of S2, than any other corner of the cell.
