Detailed Description

The arrangement of closed curves of segments and circular arcs, and the boundary of the region they wind around.

The curves are noded where they meet, and every piece between two nodes is labelled with the winding number of the curves on each side of it, by walking around the nodes as GEOS labels the depth of a buffer curve (BufferSubgraph::computeDepths). The pieces across which the winding number passes between zero and one bound the region the curves wind around.

Every question is decided exactly, or read in intervals that hold the exact value: where two segments meet and where a segment meets an arc are exact (linesegm_intersect, arcsegm_meet_exact), and so is the order of the edges around a node where two of them cross; the order of two meetings along an edge, where two arcs meet, and the order of the edges around a vertex several of them pass through are read in intervals. An interval that does not decide its question leaves the arrangement unanswered – the caller then answers by another route – so no tolerance decides anything. A point the arrangement constructs, a crossing interior to two edges, is rounded once and is a node by its identity, never located again.
