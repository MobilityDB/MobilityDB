Detailed Description

The stops of a temporal network point whose route bends between two instants, judged by the closed form of the route.

A stop is a stay whose points lie within the maximum distance of each other, and a network point passes every point of its route between two instants. The route of the network written here runs from (0 0) to (100 0) and back to (0 1), so its two ends stand 1 apart while its path reaches (100 0), 100 from the first end:

  • between the two ends no stay lies within 50, however near the ends;
  • within 150, the whole path, of diameter below 101, is one stop;
  • between the position 0 and the position 0.1, on the first leg about 20 from the start, the two instants are one stop within 50.

The route is written to the ways file tnpoint_stops_ways.csv in the current directory, removed at the end.
