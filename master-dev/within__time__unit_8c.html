Detailed Description

Test of the periods during which a temporal point or a temporal circular buffer is within a distance of a geometry, tdwithin_tgeo_geo and tdwithin_tcbuffer_geo.

Each case is built so that its answer is known in closed form, as disc_cases of ray_cast_unit.c builds its own: the coordinates are dyadic, so every bound of a period falls on a whole microsecond. A point moving along the x axis from -10 to 10 over 20 seconds, or a disc of radius 1/4 along it, is within 5/4 of the segment from (-1, 3/4) to (1, 3/4) while |x| <= 2, from second 8 to second 12; within 1 of the end (4, 1) of a segment at the single instant it passes x = 4, second 14; within 1/4 of the square of side 2 about the origin from second 8.75 to second 11.25, the instants inside it included; and, starting at the centre of that square and moving along x to 20, within 1/4 of it until second 1.25. Each runs at the scales 1, 2^-30 and 2^-40, which are exact on doubles, so an answer depends on no magnitude.
