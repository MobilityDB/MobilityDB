bool mest_span_key_answers ( int16  flags,
bool  ordered 
)

Return true if a key of a multi-entry index whose keys are spans answers a scan, ordered or not.

A set or a span set is keyed by the spans of its elements or of its components and by its extent, its bounding span, which carries the flag MEOS_FLAG_WHOLE (mest_span_keys_whole). The distance of the value is that of its extent, which a key of a piece overstates, so an ordered scan reads the key of the extent alone, which answers every strategy as the key of a single-entry index does and the distance exactly; a scan that is not ordered reads the keys of the pieces alone, which answer the strategies of mest_pieces_strategy more selectively. Each value is reported at the smallest distance among its keys, and a key of a piece of an ordered scan lies at the maximum one. The index keeps the keys of the extents and those of the pieces in subtrees of their own, an inner key over both carrying the flag MEOS_FLAG_PARTS, which answers any scan (Span_mgist_union, Span_mquadtree_choose). The keys of a temporal value keyed by spans are its pieces, whose classes hold no distance
