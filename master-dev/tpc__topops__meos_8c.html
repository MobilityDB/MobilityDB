Detailed Description

Topological bounding box operators for temporal point clouds.

These operators test the bounding boxes of temporal point clouds, which are a TPCBox, where the x, y, and optional z coordinates are for the space dimension and the t coordinate is for the time dimension.

The following operators are defined: overlaps, contains, contained, same, and adjacent.

The operators consider as many dimensions as they are shared in both arguments: only the space dimension, only the time dimension, or both the space and the time dimensions.
