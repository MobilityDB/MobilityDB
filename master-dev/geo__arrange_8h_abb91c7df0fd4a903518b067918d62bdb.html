Return the rings bounding the region closed curves of segments and arcs between tangent points wind around, as surfaces: each shell and the holes it owns.

The curves are given as their edges, segments between points where tangents touch circles (edges_add_tangent_segment) and arcs of circles of a centre and a radius between two such points of the circle (edges_add_tangent_arc), each curve's edges consecutive, each starting at the tangent point the one before ends at, and the last ending at the one the first starts at. Each edge is a stretch of the arrangement (arr_arrange_stretches): a segment between its two points, an arc as the stretch of the whole circle between them, or the two stretches either side of where the circle starts, the point furthest along -x, where it passes that point, the order of its ends around the circle decided exactly (arr_tangent_order). The arc about a vertex is therefore that vertex's circle, and arcs about one vertex lie on one circle exactly, however their ends round. Every circle of one centre and radius is held once, counterclockwise: a clockwise arc is the reversed stretch of it, winding -1, and stretches of one circle running along each other are merged into stretches whose windings add. The region is the set of points the curves wind around at least once, as arrange_wound_surfaces reads it

Parameters
[in]edgesThe edges of the curves
[out]rings,nringsThe rings, released by arrange_rings_free
[out]ownerFor every ring, the index of the shell owning it where it is a hole, -1 where it is a shell; released by the caller
