Detailed Description

Test of the owner of a boundary point of an H3 cell of meos/src/h3/h3_geo.c.

For every cell of the resolutions 0 to a maximum, the argument of the program (2 by default): each edge of its outline is held by exactly one of the two cells sharing it (h3_outline_edge_held), and each vertex, the extra vertices of the edges crossing a face of the icosahedron included, by exactly one of the cells meeting there (h3_outline_vertex_held). One owner holds whatever the sense of the outline, so the test also checks the sense the rule reads: away from the poles and the antimeridian, the centre of the cell lies left of every edge, so the edge a cell holds runs west, and is its north side.
