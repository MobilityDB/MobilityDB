DistFun *  sq

Set sq to the square of f.

For f = p + SUM_i [A_i*cos(w_i*t) + B_i*sin(w_i*t)], with p, A_i and B_i affine, the square is p^2, plus 2*p times each term, plus the products of the terms. The square of the term of the frequency u is

(A^2 + B^2)/2 + (A^2 - B^2)/2 * cos(2*u*t) + A*B * sin(2*u*t)

The result has quadratic coefficients and the frequencies of f, their doubles, and the sums and differences of every two of them, each term going to the term of its frequency, see distfun_add_cs2(). A function of the walk against a body at rest has one frequency, w, whose square has the frequencies w and 2w; a function of the walk of two bodies that turn has the frequencies wA, wB and wA - wB.

Returns
False if f has a quadratic polynomial part or quadratic coefficients, or if the square has more than DISTFUN_MAXTERMS terms
