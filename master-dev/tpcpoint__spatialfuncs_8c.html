Detailed Description

Per-point spatial predicates for the tpcpoint temporal type.

Provides the two per-event streaming entry-points (e.g. MobilityNebula NES operators): nad_tpcpoint_geo and eintersects_tpcpoint_geo. The instant constructor tpcpointinst_make lives with the rest of the Temporal<T> value surface in tpcpoint.c.
