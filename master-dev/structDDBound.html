Detailed Description

A value held as the unevaluated sum of two doubles, with a bound on its distance to the exact value it stands for, the filter of the 3D interior distance.

The sum is formed by the error-free transforms two_diff, two_product and two_sum, so it carries about 104 bits on every platform and under every emulator that rounds a double and a fused multiply-add correctly
