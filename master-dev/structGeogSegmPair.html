Detailed Description

Two geography point segments over the same period.

A geography point segment moves along the great circle of its ends at a constant angular speed, where interpolate_point4d_spheroid places it for pointsegm_interpolate: at the fraction f of the segment it stands at A cos(om f) + C sin(om f) on the unit sphere, A being its start, C the unit vector orthogonal to A in the plane of the great circle toward its end, and om the angle between its ends. A constant segment has om = 0.
