Ensure that two temporal pose chain instants hold the same number of links.

The link count of a temporal pose chain is the same at every instant: a chain that gains a joint is a different structure rather than a later value of the same one. The check holds whatever the interpolation, since it is the identity of the value that requires it and not the blend between two of them.
