Detailed Description

Frame the distance kernels read an arc edge in.

The arc is read off its chord and the inscribed angle at its middle vertex (#lw_arc_frame), as the distance of a point to an arc reads it, rather than off its circumcentre: the chord is made of input vertices, and the circumcentre of a nearly straight arc lies far from them
