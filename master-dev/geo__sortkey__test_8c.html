Detailed Description

A program that tests that geometries and geographies are ordered and equated by value, and that the sort keys of their sets and temporal values order them as their comparison does.

The comparison of two geometries reads their fields in order: the SRID, the Z, M and geodetic flags, the geometry type, so that a point precedes any other geometry, the counts of the parts, rings and vertices before what they govern, then the coordinates as numbers, a zero of either sign being 0 and a geography read on the sphere, its longitude -180 being 180 and any longitude at a pole one point. Their equality holds every field equal. A set and a temporal value hold geometries of one SRID and never an empty one, so the program reads every geometry in the SRID 0 and checks:

  • over pairs stated beside each rule, for geometries and for geographies, that the comparison answers as the rule states;
  • over every pair of a corpus of geometries, each read as the one value of a set, that the comparison answers 0 exactly when the equality holds and that swapping the operands swaps the sign, and over every triple, that it is transitive; the pairs whose comparison answers otherwise than their equality are printed;
  • over every pair of sets of one and of two geometries, and of temporal geometries of one and of two instants, that the byte order of the keys is the order of set_cmp and temporal_cmp.

The corpus holds every geometry type, geometries that share a bounding box, and geometries spelled with a coordinate -0, which is the number 0.

The program can be built as follows

gcc -Wall -g -I/usr/local/include -o geo_sortkey_test geo_sortkey_test.c -L/usr/local/lib -lmeos -lm
