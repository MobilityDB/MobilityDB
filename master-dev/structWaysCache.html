Detailed Description

Structure to represent the ways cache.

The entries are stored in an array that only grows, and the slots of the hash table, whose size is twice the capacity of the array, hold the position of an entry in it. The entries holding a geometry are linked in the order of their use, from the most recent one, the head, to the least recent one, the tail.
