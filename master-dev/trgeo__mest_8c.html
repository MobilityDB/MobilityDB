Detailed Description

Multi-entry support of the temporal rigid geometries: the keys of a value are the spatiotemporal boxes MEOS splits it into.

The extraction methods of their multi-entry classes, as tgeo_mest.c holds those of the temporal geometries; each calls the internal twin of a splitter or a merge of the temporal rigid geometries, whose boxes read the body of the value
