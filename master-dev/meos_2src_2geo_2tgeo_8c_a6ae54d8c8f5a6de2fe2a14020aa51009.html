Return a temporal point instant from the coordinates of a point and a timestamptz.

The instant tinstant_make makes from the point geopoint_make serializes, byte for byte, the point written in the instant as gserialized2_from_lwgeom writes it instead of being serialized apart, copied into the instant and freed. A point has no bounding box and no extended flags, so it is its header, its type, its number of points and its coordinates

Parameters
[in]x,y,zCoordinates, z read only when hasz is true
[in]haszTrue when the point has a Z
[in]geodeticTrue for a temporal geography point
[in]sridSRID
[in]tTimestamp
Precondition
The coordinates are finite and the SRID of a geography is geodetic, which the caller tests at its external entry
