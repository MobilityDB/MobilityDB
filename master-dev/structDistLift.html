Detailed Description

Walk of the closest features over the segments of a sequence, the parameter of the turning point functions of the lift of the temporal distance (dist_lift_temp_turnpt, dist_lift_base_turnpt)

The closest feature at the end of a segment is the one at the start of the next one. The walk keeps the instants and distances of the last segment it walked, which a second call for that segment, made with an array that holds them all, reads without walking it again. For a static geometry of several parts, the walk of each part keeps its pieces, and the instants are those of the lower envelope, see dist_lift_parts().
