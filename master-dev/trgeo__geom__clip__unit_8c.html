Detailed Description

Test of the clip of a moving edge by a polygon of meos/src/rgeo/trgeo_geom_clip.c.

A triangle moves between three 2D poses, translating and rotating, across a square. For each segment between two poses and each edge of the triangle, trgeometry_geom_clip_polygon_posed answers the intervals of the segment, as fractions in [0, 1], during which the edge meets the square. The test places the edge at NSAMPLES + 1 fractions of the segment, with the poses interpolated by posesegm_interpolate, and compares whether the fraction lies in an answered interval with whether the 2D distance of liblwgeom from the placed edge to the square is zero. A fraction closer than TOL to an end of an interval is skipped.
