Detailed Description

Closest-feature walk for the distance between rigid geometries.

The walk finds the distance between a convex polygon A and a convex polygon, a segment or a point B over one temporal segment. It follows the feature of the Minkowski difference A - B that is closest to the origin. See meos/src/rgeo/trgeo_distance.txt for the algorithm.
