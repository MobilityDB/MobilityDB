Extent aggregate of the temporal pgpointcloud types.

PG wrappers for TPCBox-based aggregate functions over the pgPointCloud temporal types — currently extent for tpcpoint / tpcpatch / tpcbox. Mirrors the stbox / tspatial extent surface in
