Settle the events of a walk at a distance of at most near on the two bodies placed by their poses, exactly.

The walk reads its functions in doubles, so that where a body turns past the other within a few units in the last place, the distance it reads may be 0 while they are apart, or a few units in the last place while they meet, at a change of feature as at an extremum or an entry. The bound near is the one below which the doubles of the walk do not separate a distance from 0. A run of events at a distance of at most near, with the overlaps between them, is decided on the bodies placed by their poses (rigid_convex_apart) over the time from the event before the run to the event after it. Where the bodies are apart over that time, each event of the run takes the double nearest the distance at its time (rigid_convex_distance), and each overlap of the run the function of the feature next to it. Where they meet, the distance 0 at a time at which they meet joins the events. Where the precision decides neither, the run keeps the values of the walk.
