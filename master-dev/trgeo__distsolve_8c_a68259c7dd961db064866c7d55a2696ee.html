int distfun_sign_after ( const DistFun *  f,
double  level,
double  t,
double  ftol 
)

Return the side of level on which f is just after t.

The walk asks this at the times of its events, where f is often exactly on the level, so the value alone cannot decide. The result is the sign of the first term of the Taylor series of f - level at t that is larger than ftol: the value, then the first derivative, then the second derivative. Thus a function that crosses the level at t gets the sign of its first derivative, and a function that touches the level gets the sign of its second derivative.

Returns
1 above the level, -1 below the level, and 0 if the three terms are all within the tolerance
