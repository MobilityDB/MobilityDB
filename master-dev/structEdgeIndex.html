Detailed Description

Index over the boxes of an edge array, answering which edges a box meets.

The edges stand in order of the bottom of their box and of its left, and a box meets an edge where the edge's box overlaps it, closed on both axes, as an R-tree of the same boxes answers. A query wider than it is tall reads the edges in order of their bottom from the bottom of the query less the height of the tallest of them; a taller one reads them in order of their left. An edge far taller, or far wider, than the rest is kept apart and read by every query along that axis, so that one long edge does not widen the reading of all the others
