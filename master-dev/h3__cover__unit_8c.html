Detailed Description

Test of the planar cover of a temporal point by H3 cells of meos/src/h3/th3index_latlng.c.

A cell holds exactly the microseconds whose exact position lies in it. The planar walk states the edge of the outline it crosses into each cell and which cell holds the crossing position, and the cover dates the crossing by the truncation of its exact parameter (segment_edge_cross_offset): where the trip lies on the edge exactly at that microsecond, the cell holding the crossing position holds it, the one left, the one entered, or at a vertex a third cell met there; where it reaches the edge after it, the cell left holds it; and a cell crossed between two microseconds holds none. The rows are trips of the acceptance set of row CW3 whose cover, as tgeompoint_to_th3index answers it and read as th3index_value_at_timestamptz reads each instant, an exact oracle in rational arithmetic sharing no code with MEOS states piece by piece, each a trip the cover dated by the parameter of the walk in doubles, the entered cell holding the instant, answered otherwise.
