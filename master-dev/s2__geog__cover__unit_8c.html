Detailed Description

Test of the geodetic cover of a temporal point by S2 cells of meos/src/s2cell/ts2cell_ops.c.

A cell holds exactly the microseconds whose exact position lies in it. A geodetic cell is the region of the sphere its outline bounds with great-circle arcs, and a geodetic position moves at constant angular speed along the great circle of its segment. The geodetic walk states an edge through each crossing and which cell holds the crossing position, and the cover dates the crossing by the truncation of its exact fraction (dggs_geodetic_edge_offset): where the trip meets the edge exactly at that microsecond, the cell holding the crossing position holds it, the one left, the one entered, or at a vertex a third cell met there; where it reaches the edge after it, the cell left holds it; and a cell crossed between two microseconds holds none. The rows are trips of the geodetic acceptance set of row CW3 and random trips, at the corners, edges and poles of the cube and across the antimeridian, whose cover, as tgeogpoint_to_ts2cell answers it and read as ts2cell_value_at_timestamptz reads each instant, an exact oracle sharing no code with MEOS states piece by piece.
