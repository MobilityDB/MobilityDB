Return the terrain ruggedness index of a raster band, pixel by pixel.

The index of PostGIS's ST_TRI: the mean of the absolute differences between a pixel and its eight neighbours

Parameters
[in]rastRaster of elevations
[in]bandNumber of the band, starting at 1
[in]customextentRaster whose extent the result takes, or NULL for the extent of rast (accepts NULL)
[in]pixeltypeName of the pixel type of the result, such as 32BF
[in]interpolate_nodataTrue to interpolate the nodata pixels first
Returns
