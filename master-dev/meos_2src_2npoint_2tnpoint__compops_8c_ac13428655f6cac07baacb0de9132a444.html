static int eaoverlaps_tnpoint_tnpoint ( const Temporal *  temp1,
const Temporal *  temp2,
bool  ever 
)
static

Return 1 if two temporal network points are ever (or always) at one place of the map at a time they share, 0 if not, and -1 on error.

Two network points are at one place where the points their routes construct for them coincide: on one route at one fraction, at a junction where one route ends and another starts, or at a crossing of two routes, the spatial equality of the two values as the equality of their geometries is to = and <>, which compare the route and the fraction. The positions are constructed points, so they coincide within the band of tnpoint_meeting_band, and the values are at one place at a time where the temporal points of their positions (tnpoint_to_tgeompoint) are within that distance (edwithin_tgeo_tgeo, adwithin_tgeo_tgeo)

Parameters
[in]temp1,temp2Temporal network points
[in]everTrue for the ever semantics, false for the always semantics
