BandStats * raster_summary_stats_finalfn ( RasterStatsState *  state)

Final function of the aggregate summarizing the pixels of rasters.

The mean is the sum over the count and the standard deviation the square root of the sum of squared deviations over the count, as PostGIS's RASTER_summaryStats_finalfn takes them when every pixel is read. The state is freed, as raster_same_alignment_finalfn frees its own
