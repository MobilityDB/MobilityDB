Datum Trgeometry_mest_adaptsplit ( PG_FUNCTION_ARGS  )

Multi-entry extract method keyed by boxes of a number of segments.

The keys of a temporal rigid geometry are the spatiotemporal boxes merged from the boxes of its instants or segments until a box holds on average a number of them, the number being the option of the class (mest_count) and the merge that of trgeometry_merge_each_n_stboxes
