Reference ellipsoids of the geographic SRIDs in common use, ordered by SRID.

Note
The values are those of the SPHEROID clause of the srtext column of the corresponding record of the spatial_ref_sys table, so that a lookup here and a lookup through PROJ agree. Every entry is a geographic coordinate system, which is what makes the table usable both for obtaining the ellipsoid and for answering whether the SRID is geodetic. Projected coordinate systems are deliberately absent: EPSG:3857 and the like are defined on an ellipsoid but are not lon/lat, and both functions below must keep reporting them as not geodetic.
