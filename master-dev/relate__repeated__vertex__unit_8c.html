Detailed Description

Test of the relationships of areas whose rings repeat a vertex, geom_relate, geom_contains and geom_covers.

A ring that repeats a vertex holds the same point set as the ring written once, so each relationship answers alike. The square from (0, 0) to (2, 2) is written with one vertex repeated at each of its positions, and in the middle of a side, against the square written once, which shares its whole boundary, in both orders: the DE-9IM matrix of two equal areas, 2FFF1FFF2, and contains and covers true. Against a larger square holding it, a square sharing one side and a square overlapping it, each matrix is the one the square written once answers. The cases mirror arc_cases of arc_cross_unit.c in being built so that their answer is known; every coordinate is a small integer, exact on doubles.
