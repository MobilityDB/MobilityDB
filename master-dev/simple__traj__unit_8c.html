Detailed Description

Test of the simplicity of a temporal point, tpoint_is_simple and tpoint_make_simple.

Each trajectory is built so that its answer is known: a path whose first and third segments cross at (1, 1) is not simple and splits in two; a zigzag of three parallel or meeting segments is simple; a hook that comes back to pass 1/4 beside its first segment is simple, and the same hook passing 1/4 beyond it crosses it once and splits in two. Each runs at the scales 1, 2^-14 and 2^-30, which are exact on doubles, so an answer depends on no magnitude: at 2^-14, segments about 10^-4 long, about 11 metres in degrees.
