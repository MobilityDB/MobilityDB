Detailed Description

The exact parts of a point of a face of an icosahedral gnomonic grid (GnomonicFacePoint), read once.

H3's formula (_hex2dToVec3 and vec3ToLatLng of its faceijk.c and vec3d.h) places the planar point p = (x, y) of a face of center c at the distance r = atan(|p| k) along the azimuth az = axis - atan2(y, x): v = cos r c + sin r (cos az n + sin az e), with n the unit north at c (the pole less its part along c, normalized), e = n x c and k the gnomonic length of the unit at the resolution. Its longitude and latitude are those of the direction of v, so a positive factor leaves them unchanged: tan r = |p| k and |p| cos az = cos(axis) x + sin(axis) y, |p| sin az = sin(axis) x - cos(axis) y turn it into v ~ |n| c + k [(cos(axis) x + sin(axis) y) n + (sin(axis) x - cos(axis) y) (n x c)] with n unnormalized. With the center C / P and the gnomonic length G 10^-g read on their literals, the point (U, W sqrt 3) / (2D) and k = 7^-m [7^-1/2] [1/3] G 10^-g at the resolution 2m [+1] of a [substrate] grid, the factor 2D 7^m [sqrt 7] [3] 10^g P^3 leaves v ~ f1 [sqrt 7] |N| C + G P a N + G b (N x C), with N = P^2 n integer, a = cos(axis) U + sin(axis) W sqrt 3, b = sin(axis) U - cos(axis) W sqrt 3 and f1 = 2D 7^m [3] 10^g: integers but for sqrt 3, sqrt 7, |N| and the sine and cosine of the axis, which gnomonic_vector encloses at each precision
