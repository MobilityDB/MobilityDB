Detailed Description

A segment of a temporal geography point moving along a great circle, and the vertices and the edges of a geography on the unit sphere.

The segment stands at A cos(om f) + C sin(om f) at the fraction f of it, as in GeogSegmPair. An edge of the geography is the minor arc of the great circle of its ends, of pole pole as #unit_normal states it. The cosine of the angle from the segment to a vertex, the sine of the angle to the great circle of an edge, and the sides of the two planes bounding the lune over which the nearest point of an edge lies inside it are each a sinusoid a cos(om f) + b sin(om f) of the fraction.
