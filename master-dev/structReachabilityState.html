Detailed Description

State of the reachability filter of a stream of reports.

The reports are walked in the order of their times: a report is kept when the last kept report could reach it at the maximum speed. The walk trusts its first report, so its seed is chosen among the first REACH_HEAD reports, which the state holds until they have arrived, and the reports before the seed are walked backwards from it. The state counts the reports, those unreachable from the report before, and the kept ones, and holds the kept reports not yet handed back by reachabilitystate_instants.
