Temporal * temporal_reachability_filter ( const Temporal *  temp,
double  maxspeed 
)

Return a temporal value with the instants a reachability walk keeps.

The instants of the value are walked in the order of their times: an instant is kept when the last kept instant could reach it at the maximum speed, the distance being the difference of two floats, the planar distance of two geometry points, or the distance on the spheroid of two geography points. The walk trusts its first instant, so its seed is the earliest of the first REACH_HEAD instants that a majority of them can reach, and the instants before the seed are walked backwards from it. The value is dropped whole when more than half of its instants are unreachable from the instant before. The kept instants keep their sequences and the interpolation of the value, as temporal_ext_kalman_filter keeps them.

Parameters
[in]tempTemporal value
[in]maxspeedMaximum speed, in units of the values per second
Returns
The kept instants, NULL when the value is dropped whole
