Detailed Description

Cache of the routes of the ways network.

Both builds keep the routes they read in this cache, the MEOS build in ways_meos.c from the ways CSV file and the PostgreSQL build in ways.c from the ways table, and differ only in how a route is read and in where the cache lives.

A route is found by its identifier through a hash table, so that the cost of a lookup does not grow with the size of the network. The cache is bound by the bytes of the geometries it holds, not by its number of routes, since the geometry of a route ranges from a few tens of bytes to several kilobytes. When the bound is reached the geometry of the route used least recently is released, while its entry and its length are kept: an entry costs a few tens of bytes, and keeping it answers the existence and the length of every route already read without reading it again, whatever the size of the network.
