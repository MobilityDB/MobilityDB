Detailed Description

The endpoints of the curves of a linear geometry, sorted by their coordinates.

The sort lets the endpoints a point equals be found by a binary search rather than by a scan of every edge. The endpoints are those relate_point_on_linear_boundary counts: both ends of every non-empty straight or circular linear edge. An endpoint with a coordinate that is not a number equals no point, so it is left out
