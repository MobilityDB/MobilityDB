Return how many cells of the dense walk the th3index cover of the same segment does not hold, or -1 where a conversion answers nothing.

A conversion that fails answers NULL, and counting that as "no cell is missing" reports a cover that was never built as a sound one, so it is carried back as its own answer rather than folded into the count.
