43/*****************************************************************************
44 * Motion in one temporal segment
45 *
46 * In a segment, the time t goes from 0 to 1. A body translates linearly and
47 * rotates at a constant rate about its rotation center. A point a of the
48 * body, given relative to the rotation center, moves as
49 *
50 * v(t) = R(th0 + w*t) * a + (cx, cy) + t * (dx, dy)
51 *
52 * where R(x) is the rotation by the angle x. A static geometry has w = 0 and
53 * (dx, dy) = 0.
54 *****************************************************************************/
55

157/*****************************************************************************
158 * The walk
159 *
160 * A is a convex polygon, and B is a convex polygon, a segment or a point.
161 * The distance between A and B is the distance from the origin to the
162 * Minkowski difference A - B. The features of the difference are pairs of
163 * features of A and B:
164 *
165 * VV(i,j) the vertex a_i - b_j
166 * EV(i,j) the edge from a_i - b_j to a_{i+1} - b_j
167 * VE(i,j) the edge from a_i - b_j to a_i - b_{j+1}
168 *
169 * If B is a point, the difference is A moved by -b_0 and has no VE edge.
170 *****************************************************************************/
171
