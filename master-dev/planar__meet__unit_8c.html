Detailed Description

Two planar temporal points that meet between two microseconds.

A runs from (-h 0) to (h 0) and B from (0 -h) to (0 h) over the same D microseconds, so both lie at (0 0) at D/2 and they meet for every D; an odd D puts the meeting at a half microsecond. In 3D B also rises from -h to h, and still meets A at the origin. The controls move B to x = 1e-6, where the two never meet. ever_eq_tgeo_tgeo and eintersects_tgeo_tgeo read time as continuous, so they answer true on every meeting and false on every control.
