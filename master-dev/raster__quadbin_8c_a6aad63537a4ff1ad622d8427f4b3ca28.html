Return the values a raster holds along a linearly interpolated sequence, as the sequences of a step temporal float.

A trajectory moving between two instants passes over the pixels between them, and their values belong to the answer as much as the values under the instants themselves. The segment is therefore walked in steps of step, the way tpointseq_densify_to_th3index() walks one for a hexagon, and a value is emitted where it differs from the one before it: a pixel value holds until the trip reaches a pixel holding another, which is step interpolation. A position outside the raster or over a nodata pixel carries no value and ends the run, so a trip leaving and re-entering the raster answers one sequence per visit.

Parameters
[in]seqTrajectory sequence with linear interpolation
[in]boxBounding box of the raster used as a pre-filter, may be NULL
[in]sampleCallback returning the pixel value at a position
[in]ctxOpaque context passed through to the callback
[in]stepDistance between two walk positions, in the units of the trajectory
[out]resultSequences of the answer, appended from nseqs
[in,out]nseqsNumber of sequences written
