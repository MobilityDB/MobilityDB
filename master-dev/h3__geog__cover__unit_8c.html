Detailed Description

Test of the geodetic cover of a temporal point by H3 cells of meos/src/h3/th3index_latlng.c.

A cell holds exactly the microseconds whose exact position lies in it. A geodetic cell is the region of the sphere its outline bounds with great-circle arcs, and a geodetic position moves at constant angular speed along the great circle of its segment. The geodetic walk states an edge through each crossing and which cell holds the crossing position, and the cover dates the crossing by the truncation of its exact fraction (h3_geodetic_edge_offset): where the trip meets the edge exactly at that microsecond, the cell holding the crossing position holds it, the one left, the one entered, or at a vertex a third cell met there; where it reaches the edge after it, the cell left holds it; and a cell crossed between two microseconds holds none. The rows are trips of the acceptance set of row CW3 whose cover, as tgeogpoint_to_th3index answers it and read as th3index_value_at_timestamptz reads each instant, an exact oracle sharing no code with MEOS states piece by piece, each a trip the cover dated by the fraction of the walk in doubles answers otherwise.
