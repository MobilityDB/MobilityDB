Detailed Description

The three quadratics and the projection whose signs say whether a disc moving linearly over a segment of D microseconds is within a distance of a segment a b at a whole microseconds of it.

The disc is within at the fraction f where the distance of its centre c(f) to the segment is at most L(f), the radius plus the distance: within of the end a (qa), of the end b (qb), or of the line of the segment (qp) where the projection of the centre lies on the segment, 0 <= (c - a) . u <= |u|^2 with u = b - a. Over the line the quadratic is k(f)^2 - |u|^2 L(f)^2, k = u x (c - a), which is |u|^2 times the one of the disc against its foot on the line
