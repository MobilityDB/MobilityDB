Bounding Box Operations

These operations compare the bounding boxes of their arguments: the span of a set or of a span set, or the box of a temporal value, tbox, stbox or tpcbox.

Topological Operations

Whether two bounding boxes overlap, contain one another, are adjacent or are the same. The last rows test the time span of a temporal JSONB value and the route identifiers of network points.
