Comparisons

  • Traditional comparisons

    trgeometry {=, <>, <, >, <=, >=} trgeometry → boolean

    SELECT trgeometry 'Polygon((0 0,1 0,1 1,0 1,0 0));
    [Pose(Point(0 0), 0.0)@2001-01-01, Pose(Point(10 0), 0.0)@2001-01-02]' = 
  trgeometry 'Polygon((0 0,1 0,1 1,0 1,0 0));
    [Pose(Point(0 0), 0.0)@2001-01-01, Pose(Point(10 0), 0.0)@2001-01-02]';
-- t

    [Pose(Point(0 0), 0.0)@2001-01-01, Pose(Point(10 0),0.0)@2001-01-02]' <> 
  trgeometry 'Polygon((0 0,1 0,1 1,0 1,0 0));
    [Pose(Point(0 0), 0.0)@2001-01-01, Pose(Point(10 0), 0.0)@2001-01-02]';
